Our Story

Chez Lee was born from a love of authentic Ghanaian flavours and a desire to share them with the community. Every dish is crafted with fresh ingredients and genuine passion.

🌿

Fresh Ingredients

We source our produce locally every morning — market-fresh fish, farm vegetables, and real palm fruit for our soups and stews.

👨‍🍳

Home-Style Cooking

Our recipes are passed down through generations. We cook the way a Ghanaian grandmother cooks — with time, care, and no shortcuts.

🚀

Fast Delivery

We know hunger doesn't wait. Our delivery team covers Koforidua quickly so your food arrives hot, fresh, and on time — every time.

♻️

Eco Packaging

We use biodegradable packaging wherever possible because good food should also mean a cleaner planet.
